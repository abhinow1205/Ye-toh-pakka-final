      5 Business Processes You Should Automate Before Hiring More People

      Before adding another person to your team, ask yourself: is the workload actually growing, or is your team simply spending too much time on repetitive work?

      Sam RuizStaff Engineer, Data Platform
      Aug 1, 2026 3 min read
      Reporting & Data Collection Lead Management & Follow-Ups Routine Customer Communication Data Entry & System Updates Internal Workflows & Approvals

      Hiring is often the obvious solution when a team becomes overloaded. But not every workload problem requires another employee. Many businesses spend significant amounts of time on repetitive tasks that follow the same steps every day — collecting data, sending updates, creating reports, moving information between systems, or following up with customers. These are exactly the kinds of processes that can often be automated. Here are five business processes worth looking at before expanding your team.

      Reporting & Data Collection

      If your team regularly copies data from different platforms into spreadsheets to create reports, you may have an automation opportunity. Sales numbers, marketing performance, operational metrics, financial information, and other business data can often be collected automatically and presented through a centralized dashboard.

      Instead of spending hours preparing the report, your team can spend that time understanding what the numbers actually mean.

      "Automate the collection. Let people focus on the analysis. "

      Lead Management & Follow-Ups

      A new lead shouldn't require someone to manually update multiple systems, assign the lead, send an acknowledgement, and remember to follow up. An automated workflow can move a new enquiry through predefined steps.

      Your team still handles the conversation and relationship, but the repetitive administrative work happens automatically. This becomes particularly valuable when lead volume starts increasing.

      Routine Customer Communication

      Businesses often send the same types of messages repeatedly — enquiry confirmations, appointment reminders, order updates, follow-up emails, feedback requests, or internal notifications.

      If the message and trigger are predictable, the process may not need to be handled manually every time. Automation can trigger the appropriate communication based on an event, while your team focuses on conversations that actually require human attention.

      Data Entry & System Updates

      One of the biggest sources of repetitive work is entering the same information into multiple systems. For example, a team member may receive information through a form, enter it into a spreadsheet, update a CRM, and then notify another department.

      If the same information is simply being moved from one place to another, there is often little value in having a person do it manually. Automation can connect these systems and move information between them automatically. Less copying. Fewer errors. More time for productive work.

      Internal Workflows & Approvals

      Many internal processes depend on someone remembering to send an email, request approval, update a status, or notify the next person in a workflow. These small tasks may not seem significant individually, but across an organization they can consume hundreds of hours.

      Processes such as expense approvals, document reviews, task assignments, onboarding steps, and internal requests can often be structured into automated workflows. The system can determine what happens next, who needs to be notified, and when an action is required.

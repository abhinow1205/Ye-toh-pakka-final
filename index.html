      Case study

      Northbridge rebrand

      A full identity overhaul for a 40-year-old finance firm.

      Overview

      A century-old feel, a modern-day mark

      The brief called for a system that felt established but not stale — new type, a restrained palette, and a mark that reads at both business-card and billboard scale.

      Approach

      Audit, then rebuild from the inside out

      We started with a full audit of every touchpoint, from letterhead to lobby signage, then rebuilt the identity around a small set of flexible rules the internal team could actually maintain.

      Deliverables

      A system built to hand off

      We shipped a full identity system, a 90-page brand guideline, and a set of motion tokens for their product team to build from.

      Results

      Adopted company-wide within a quarter

      Every business unit had migrated to the new system within one quarter, with internal teams reporting far fewer one-off design requests once the guidelines shipped.

      Team

      A small, senior team

      Two brand designers, one motion designer, and a strategist worked directly with Northbridge's marketing lead over a twelve-week engagement.

      Case study

      Solstice app redesign

      Cutting checkout friction across mobile and web.

      Overview

      Same flow, mobile and web

      Solstice needed one checkout experience that felt native on both mobile and web, without maintaining two separate flows in parallel.

      Problem

      Checkout was the bottleneck

      Three rounds of usability testing pointed to the same drop-off point: checkout. Too many steps, vague error states, and no visible order summary once payment began.

      Solution

      Fewer steps, clearer feedback

      We rebuilt the flow around fewer steps, clearer inline error states, and a persistent order summary that stayed visible through every stage of payment.

      Results

      Abandonment dropped within a month

      Cart abandonment dropped meaningfully within the first month post-launch, and support tickets tagged "checkout" fell sharply in the same window.

      Team

      Design paired with research

      One product designer and one researcher ran the testing rounds, working alongside Solstice's in-house engineering team through implementation.

      Case study

      Ferrovia dashboard

      Real-time fleet monitoring for 400+ vehicles.

      Overview

      A calm view over a busy fleet

      Ferrovia needed a single dashboard for dispatchers to monitor over 400 vehicles in real time, without the screen turning into noise.

      Challenge

      Too much data, not enough signal

      Dispatchers needed to track hundreds of vehicles at once, but earlier prototypes buried the vehicles that actually needed attention under everything else.

      Design

      A layered map, disclosed on demand

      We designed a layered map view with progressive disclosure, so the default state stays calm and any vehicle's detail is only ever a click away.

      Outcome

      Faster response, fewer missed alerts

      Dispatchers reported faster response times to flagged vehicles, and the layered view held up even during peak-hour load with the full fleet active.

      Team

      Design and engineering, in lockstep

      A product designer and two frontend engineers worked in weekly cycles with Ferrovia's dispatch operators to validate each iteration.

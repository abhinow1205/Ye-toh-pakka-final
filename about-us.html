Our story

Five years, one steady idea.

Automation shouldn't require an engineering team to set up. That belief hasn't changed since day one — everything else has just gotten better at proving it.

2021

Founded in a two-bedroom apartment. The first version of the automation engine was built and shipped over a single weekend.

2022

Crossed 1,000 paying teams and shipped the visual workflow builder — no code required to connect two tools together.

2023

Raised our Series A and crossed 10 million automated tasks run through the platform in a single year.

2024

Launched the enterprise tier and opened our first office — still small enough that everyone knows everyone.

2026

Today, over 40,000 teams across 120 countries run their busywork through Lumen. We're just getting started.

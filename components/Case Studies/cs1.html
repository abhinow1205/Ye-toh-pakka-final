What we built instead

We replaced the manual monitoring process with a Python engine that automatically scans 1000+ news sources every hour, flags relevant stories, and forwards them to the concerned person in real time.

01 — Automated ingestion

A live feed pulls from thousands of news sources every minute, no human watching required.

02 — Keyword-based filtering

Custom Python logic scans every article for client-defined keywords and topics in real time.

03 — Smart relevance scoring

Stories are ranked and filtered before a human ever sees them, cutting out the noise.

04 — Instant routing

Matched stories are automatically forwarded to the right person the moment they're detected.

05 — Always-on coverage

The system runs continuously, catching stories the old four-screen setup would have missed overnight.

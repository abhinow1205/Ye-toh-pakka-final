What we built instead

A connected pipeline that pulls data automatically, validates it in real time, and pushes finished dashboards to the people who need them.

01 — Centralized task list

Every task and its live status sit in one system, visible to the whole team.

02 — Automated approval routing

Tasks needing sign-off are automatically sent to the right senior, no chasing required.

03 — One-click approvals

Seniors review and approve directly within the system, from anywhere.

04 — Automatic execution on approval

The moment a task is approved, the system carries it out with no manual step.

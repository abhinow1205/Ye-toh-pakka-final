What we care about

Four things we don't compromise on.

Values are easy to write down and hard to hold onto once a company grows. These are the four we check ourselves against.

01

Clarity

If a workflow needs a manual to understand, we haven't finished designing it.

02

Momentum

We'd rather ship a smaller thing this week than a perfect thing next quarter.

03

Trust

Your data and your workflows are yours. We build like someone's checking.

04

Craft

Automation should feel invisible. That takes far more care than it looks like.

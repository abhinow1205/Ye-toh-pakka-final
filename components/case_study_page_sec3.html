The Friction

The Legacy Operational Bottleneck

Critical decision-makers were operating blind due to severe data lag, manual entry fatigue, and fragmented system architectures.

18+ Hours Wasted Weekly

Senior analysts spent over two full working days every week manually exporting CSV files, cleaning messy formatting, and stitching datasets together in Excel.

Disconnected Silos

Sales data lived in Salesforce, dispatch data in custom legacy SQL servers, and financial projections in Google Sheets. Zero cross-talk existed between platforms.

Frequent Human Error

Manual copy-pasting introduced recurring formula breaks and data inaccuracies, causing executive reports to contain conflicting KPI numbers.

Reporting Delays

Weekly operational reviews relied on data that was already 4 to 5 days stale, preventing proactive responses to supply chain disruptions.
